Introduction & Overview

CivicSetu bridges the gap between citizens and civic authorities by turning everyday issues—potholes, broken streetlights, drainage problems—into structured, trackable reports.

Core idea
One report → one ticket → visible lifecycle.
Proof
Geo-tag + photo evidence for accuracy.
Trust
